# Brief: Fox Lake: Indiana's Lake of Its Own (REST-01)

**Status:** APPROVED by BAM 2026-11-01 (series brief `plans/94-black-resort-towns-series-brief.md`, "BAM's
answers, 2026-11-01": all ten recommendations). Generated from that series brief per the course-brief rule;
the series brief wins where the two differ. Public-ready, held until the Fox Lake Preservation Foundation has
had about three weeks to review (answer 4). Tracked twin: `cloud-briefs/rest-01/00-approved-brief.md`.
**Slug:** `fox-lake-indianas-lake-of-its-own` · **Series:** `places-of-our-own`, code REST, position 01.

## 1. Description

A learner can tell how a lake resort for Black families was assembled in Indiana in the 1920s (who bought the
land, who sold the lots, who built the cottages), read a National Register nomination and a Green Book listing
as evidence, say what changed after 1964 and why Fox Lake held when coastal Black resorts did not, and plan a
visit that stays on public ground.

## 2. Outline (built 2026-11-01; six beats: Then, Built, Who decided, Now, Next, Write)

- **Section 1 · Then:** why a lake of its own (exclusion, Sager Lake 1922, the Klan years); 1924, five white
  businessmen and a company in Marion; Marion, 1930 (content note, no images); 1926 to 1932, three dates for
  three events (plat 1926 or 1927, first cottage 1928, the Boyds 1932); who came and what they did there; key
  terms; quiz.
- **Section 2 · Built:** the lake cottage as a building type; who built what (the company, Emery Boyd's twelve
  cottages, John A. Roberts, the hotel, barn and clubhouse, two Black women running the hotel from 1943, the
  Mar-Fran, Pryor's); quiz.
- **Section 3 · Who decided:** a private plat and an owners' association (1938, incorporated 1952); the Green
  Book as a listing, not a guarantee; designation as an instrument (2001, 2024, 2026); reading a nomination
  that disagrees with itself; quiz.
- **Section 4 · Now:** 1964 and after; now (360 tour, forthcoming), public stops only; map.
- **Section 5 · Next:** read the current record (exercise).
- **Section 6 · Write:** assignment (one cottage, one owner, one document; no private homes); what was cut and
  the unopened sources; final quiz.

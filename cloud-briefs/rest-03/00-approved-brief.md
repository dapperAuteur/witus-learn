## 1. Description

A learner can explain why Black Midwesterners bought lake land in the 1920s and 1930s, using Chicago's beaches as
the cause, and compare four lake communities that are each too thinly documented to stand alone.

## 2. Outline (DRAFT, about 18 lessons)

1. Why the lakes: Chicago, July 27, 1919 (content note), the course's opening, kept here; West Michigan Resort
   1908 to 1915; a pointer to REST-05 (`belmont-beach-and-the-citys-water`, lesson `bb-wade-ins-and-carousels`)
   for the 1960 and 1961 Rainbow Beach wade-ins; quiz.
2. Lake Ivanhoe, Wisconsin (1926): the Ryan Farm, the pavilion, the 1934 fence and the lawsuit, the 2022 marker;
   quiz.
3. Woodland Park and the Michigan list: the signage trail; the Michigan Green Book survey; Appendix A names.
4. Lake Placid, Missouri (1934 to 1937): research first; one source only. Drop if no second source.
5. Ohio: the Cabin Club in Cuyahoga Valley; Clearview Golf Club.
6. Map; assignment; final. (Rainbow Beach's 1960 and 1961 wade-ins moved to REST-05 lesson 21 by BAM's
   decision of 2026-11-01, plan 95 §12 answer 3; this course links there from section 1.)

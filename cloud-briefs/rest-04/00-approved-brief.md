# Brief: Mountain, Gulf and Shore: Lincoln Hills, Gulfside, Atlantic Beach, Carr's and Sparrow's (REST-04)

## 1. Description

A learner can compare four legal shapes a Black resort took (a lodge on private land, a church assembly, an
incorporated Black town, a family beach business) and report a place whose sources disagree without guessing.

## 2. Outline (DRAFT, about 20 lessons)

1. Lincoln Hills and Winks Panorama, Colorado: NRHP 1980 and 2014; NHL 2023; private land; the 1925 or 1928
   opening (the Green Book route course's map point now uses 1928 and shows 1925).
2. Gulfside Assembly, Mississippi (1923): a church-owned resort; the land purchase (Kahrl & Cammeron, 2022, p.
   62); Katrina; graves on site (no 360 of graves).
3. Atlantic Beach, South Carolina (1934; incorporated 1966): a Black-governed town with a public beach.
4. Carr's and Sparrow's Beaches, Annapolis: condemnation for a sewage plant (1971); the 2022 buyback.
5. Method lessons: SANS and Sag Harbor (read from the 2019 nomination only); Bay Shore, Hampton (five founding
   years).
6. Map, assignment, final.

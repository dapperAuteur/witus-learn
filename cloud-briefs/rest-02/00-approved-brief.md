# Brief: Idlewild After 1921: The Summer Apollo, the Long Decline, the Fourth Generation (REST-02)

**Status:** APPROVED by BAM 2026-11-01 (series brief `plans/94-black-resort-towns-series-brief.md`, §4 and
"BAM's answers"). Generated from the series brief per the course-brief rule. NOT BUILT. Public-ready, held
until the Idlewild Historic & Cultural Center has had about three weeks to review. Tracked twin:
`cloud-briefs/rest-02/00-approved-brief.md`.

## 1. Description

Picks up where `where-we-rested` lesson 4 stops. A learner can explain how a lot-sales resort became an
entertainment economy, how class divided it, why "integration" turned into what a township supervisor called
"disintegration" (Kahrl & Cammeron, 2022, p. 136), and how a community holds a National Register district of
1979 and 2010 today.

## 2. Outline (DRAFT, about 18 lessons)

1. Recap and the class question: read lesson 4 first; elites and the Depression influx; Du Bois on "city
   habits" (p. 57); quiz.
2. The Summer Apollo: fourteen hotels, nine nightclubs (pp. 78 to 79); the Paradise Club, reopened 1952; Braggs
   and Giles; the *Idlewild Revue* as an export business (p. 188); rule: a performer is named only from a dated
   primary or as "named by" a source; quiz.
3. Woodland Park, fifteen miles off: the Authers and the second community; the shuttle cars; quiz.
4. Disintegration: after 1964 (p. 141); co-occurrence drill.
5. Designation and now: 1979 and 2010, the NHL "high potential" finding; now (360, forthcoming); map.
6. Write: assignment; what was cut; final.

# Brief: Where We Rested, retro-coded as REST-00

**Status:** APPROVED by BAM 2026-11-01 (series brief `plans/94-black-resort-towns-series-brief.md`, answers 1, 2
and 9). Generated from the series brief per the course-brief rule. The course exists (built 2026-07-24, before
the brief rule, so exempt); this brief records the decision to make it the series' opening course and the
corrections owed. Tracked twin: `cloud-briefs/rest-00/00-approved-brief.md`.
**Slug:** `where-we-rested` · **Series:** `places-of-our-own`, code REST, position 00.

## 1. Description

Unchanged: a learner can read five Black resort and beach communities (Highland Beach, Idlewild, American Beach,
Oak Bluffs, Bruce's Beach) as property, business and government, under the rule "never use a positive to soften
a negative; the claim is co-occurrence." As REST-00 it also sets the method every later REST course applies.

## 2. Outline

No new lessons. Changes: (a) series code REST-00; (b) the Idlewild boundary increase settled as 2010 (NRIS
09001062); (c) the 1921 Idlewild Lot Owners Association re-sourced or marked as the community's own account;
(d) a pointer in lesson 21 to REST-01 Fox Lake. (b) and (c) shipped on
`fix/where-we-rested-idlewild-boundary-and-winks`; (a) and (d) ride with REST-01 because the series-code
guard refuses a one-course series coded 00.

## 3. Fit

Culture & History; public; high school and up. Prerequisite `green-book-how-to-read-a-route`. Opening course
of the REST series.

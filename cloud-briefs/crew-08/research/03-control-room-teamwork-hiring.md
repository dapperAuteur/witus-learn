# The Show Crew: The Room, the Floor, Teamwork on Comms, and Getting Hired

**Researched:** October 26, 2026.

**Scope.** Outline sections 1 (the room and the chain of command), 6 (the floor and the support crew), 7 (how it fits together) and 8 (getting hired) of `cloud-briefs/crew-08/00-approved-brief.md`. Audio, video, replay and graphics chairs (sections 2 to 5) are covered by other packs; they appear here only where a source ties them to the chain of command, comms, checks or hiring.

**Tags.** **V** = read on October 26, 2026: fetched that day, or in a posting saved that day from the employer's public job API (each saved file's second line is its URL). **I** = inferred, with the reasoning shown. **A** = assumed; never to be taught as fact.

**How to read a posting fact.** A job posting is a primary source for what one employer asked on one date. Every pay figure is "as posted," never a market rate. Every NFL posting read today states its pay as a bare dollar figure followed by "USD," with no unit (no "per hour," "per week" or "per year"); this pack never adds one. The Fox postings state "per hour." Quoted text is copied exactly; where a source used an en or em dash, this pack writes "to" or describes the dash outside the quotation marks instead of quoting the dash.

**How the evidence was gathered.** Saved postings (read today from the employers' public job APIs): ten NFL Media postings in `scratchpad/gh/` and eight Fox postings in `scratchpad/gh/fox/`, plus five more NFL postings read today from the saved NFL Greenhouse API file `gh/nfl.json` (its `first_published` field gives each posting's first publish date). Fetched today: the Directors Guild of America's Freelance Live and Tape Television Agreement (FLTTA) Articles 1 and 2, its 2026 to 2027 network rate cards for ABC, CBS and NBC, two safety-training pages, a rate-card news item and three event write-ups; ESPN's 2018 *ESPN3 / ESPN+ School Productions Broadcast Manual* (a public PDF on a college conference's site); Clear-Com's partyline reference e-book; a Youngstown State University directing handout; a California State University, Dominguez Hills personnel handout; two Montgomery County Public Schools classroom handouts; Game Creek Video, Mobile TV Group and NEP Group pages and one spec sheet; four NABET-CWA pages; and four Sports Video Group (SVG) articles (trade press, secondary).

**Source weight.** DGA agreement text and rate cards, ESPN's manual, the postings and maker pages are primary or near-primary. The university and school handouts are teaching documents, named as such; two are old (2003 and 2007) and one is a middle or high school classroom handout. SVG is trade press and is named as secondary wherever it is used.

### 7.1 Comms: channels and who hears whom

| Fact | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|

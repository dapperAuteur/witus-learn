**Researched:** October 26, 2026.

**Scope.** Outline sections 3 (video: the V1 and the V2), 4 (replay and playout) and 5 (graphics) of `cloud-briefs/crew-08/00-approved-brief.md`. Audio, the floor crew, comms and the director's calls are other packs' scope. This pack records what employers and makers say each chair does and what gear it runs. It does not teach a maker's operator screens.

**Tags.** **V** = read on October 26, 2026, either fetched that day or in a posting saved from the employer's public job API that day (marked "saved October 26, 2026"). **I** = inferred, with the reasoning shown. **A** = assumed; never to be taught as fact.

**How to read a posting fact.** A job posting is a primary source for what one employer asked on one date. Every pay figure below is "as posted" with its date, never a market rate. The NFL's Greenhouse postings print a bare dollar figure or range followed by "USD" with no unit (no "per hour," no "per year"); this pack says so each time. Fox, Disney and the 2023 Turner Sports posting state a unit, and this pack gives it. Quoted text is copied exactly; where a posting used an en or em dash, this pack writes "to" outside the quotation marks instead of quoting the dash.

**Which postings are sports.** The NFL postings are NFL Media (Inglewood, California). The Fox Viz Operator, Senior Video Operator, Playout Operator and Real-Time Graphics Artist are **Fox News Media** postings in New York: news control rooms, not sports trucks. They are used here because they are the live, public postings for those chairs on October 26, 2026, and each lesson must say they are news postings. The Fox internship and the Fox video transmission engineer are Fox Corporation postings for Fox Sports work.

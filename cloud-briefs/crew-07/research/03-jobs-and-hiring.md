# The Sideline's Other Crew: Jobs, Game Day and Hiring Facts

**Researched:** October 26, 2026.

**Scope.** Outline sections 4 (the game day, hour by hour, from the postings), 5 (the jobs and the ladder) and 6 (getting hired) of `cloud-briefs/crew-07/00-approved-brief.md`. Medicine is out of scope: this pack records what postings ask of non-medical staff and who they support. It never describes how an injury is assessed.

**Tags.** **V** = read on October 26, 2026, either fetched that day or in a page the publisher saved in a browser that day (marked "saved October 26, 2026"). **I** = inferred, with the reasoning shown. **A** = assumed; never to be taught as fact.

**How to read a posting fact.** A job posting is a primary source for what one employer asked on one date. Every pay figure below is "as posted" with its date, never a market rate. Quoted text is copied exactly; where a posting used an en or em dash, this pack writes "to" outside the quotation marks instead of quoting the dash.

**How the evidence was gathered.** Saved pages: the NFL Injury Video Review Operator (SHRM HireVets copy), the Hawk-Eye Rutgers technician (startup.jobs copy, closed), the Hawk-Eye SEC coordinator, the Hawk-Eye UK expression of interest, Hawk-Eye's careers home page, Hawk-Eye's TeamWork Online and startup.jobs lists, and the NFL's Greenhouse board. Fetched today: the NFL Greenhouse API (42 postings), the HireVets page itself (for its embedded dates), Hawk-Eye's Ashby public job board API (25 postings, with publish dates), the NBA's Workday board, the Greenhouse boards of KINEXON, Genius Sports and Catapult, Sportradar's SmartRecruiters board, and three Microsoft support pages.

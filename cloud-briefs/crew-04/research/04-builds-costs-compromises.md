**Why this tier is the balanced middle for sports [I]:** on network shows the RF, receive antennas and shading usually come from the network's vendors (Costa, 2026; Mrozek, 2021) [V]; see `02`. Going higher (section 2.3) only makes sense if a show asks for SDI from the camera, genlock, 12G or 4K.

## 5. Compromises worth making

| Compromise | Why it is worth it |
|---|---|
| **Own the camera core; rent or partner for network RF.** | Network shows bring vendor RF, receive antennas and coordination (Costa, 2026; Hernández, 2026) [V]; licensed channels sit under network authority (Eligibility for License, 2026) [V]. Buying broadcast RF without a show that needs it is the most expensive mistake available [I]. |
| **A prosumer 5 GHz link for film, documentaries, video village and small streams.** | There the link is monitoring or the show accepts a few frames of delay; the Accsoon Master 4K specifies "below 25ms at 1080p60" (Accsoon, n.d.-b) [V], the Cosmo C2 "≥ 33ms" (Hollyland, n.d.-c) [V]. Saves $1,391 (Cosmo C2 at $999) to $1,541 (CineView Master 4K at $849) against the $2,390 Bolt 6 LT set [I]. |
| **f/2.8 zoom as the working lens; f/1.2 prime for set pieces.** | At 3 m a 50mm at f/2.8 holds about 0.60 m in focus versus 0.26 m at f/1.2 (see `01`) [I]; Fox's crews already work under 2 feet of depth (Mrozek, 2021) [V]. |
| **Sigma 24-70 DG DN II instead of Sony GM II.** | Saves $1,229 ($2,448 versus $1,219) (Focus Camera, n.d.-g; Sigma Corporation of America, n.d.-a) [V/I]. Rent both for one game before deciding; autofocus behavior on moving players is the test [I]. |
| **Used FX3 from a dealer instead of a new FX3A.** | Saves $369 to $749 (MPB, n.d.) [V/I]. Check that remote control works: Cyanview lists different minimum firmware for the FX3 and the FX3A (Cyanview, n.d.-b) [V]. |
| **98 to 99 Wh batteries instead of larger packs.** | Under 100 Wh, spares fly in carry-on without airline approval; 101 to 160 Wh needs approval and is limited to two spares (Transportation Security Administration [TSA], n.d.) [V]. More swaps, easier travel [I]. |
| **Internal recording instead of an external recorder.** | Fewer cables on the gimbal; add a recorder only when a show requires a format the FX3 cannot record [I]. |
| **Portkeys at 2000 nits instead of SmallHD at 3000.** | $399 versus $2,249 (Portkeys, n.d.; SmallHD, n.d.-b) [V]. Accept a dimmer screen and HDMI only; check stock first [I]. |

## 6. Compromises not worth making

| Compromise | Why not |
|---|---|
| **A consumer or prosumer link on a network sideline without the EIC's and RF coordinator's approval.** | The NFL prohibits uncoordinated devices and can revoke credentials (NFL Football Operations, n.d.) [V]. Unlicensed devices "must accept interference from authorized users" (Price & Juniet, 2015) [V]. A crowd blocks 5 GHz; Teradek's own guide warns against human obstructions (Teradek, 2024) [V]. One dropout on air costs the show more than the link saved [I]. |
| **Buying a link by its advertised range.** | Every range figure above is line of sight [V]; a sideline route is not [I]. |
| **An unregulated D-Tap dummy cable.** | The FX3's battery is rated 7.2 V (Sony, n.d.) [V]; D-Tap runs well above that [I]. Use a regulated cable such as the Indipro unit ($64.99) (Indipro Tools, n.d.) [V]. |
| **No hot-swap bridge, then promising "all-day" power.** | Battery count is energy, not continuity; a swap reboots everything on the pack unless something bridges it [I]. |
| **Choosing the gimbal by payload alone.** | Payload ratings do not prove balance or arm clearance with your cage, monitor and cables [I]. Rent the exact combination for a game first. |
| **Standardizing on DJI without a parts plan.** | DJI's US store lists the RS 4 and RS 4 Pro out of stock and says the RS 5 is not for sale in the United States (DJI, n.d.-b, n.d.-e) [V]. Buy a spare grip and know the repair route [I]. |
| **Buying the high tier before a show requires SDI, genlock or 12G.** | It roughly doubles the cost ($25,700.90 versus $13,556.94) [I] and does not by itself add licensed RF or shading, which networks supply. |
| **A global-shutter or Ronin 4D build as a first purchase.** | Different color pipeline, weight and price; the Ronin 4D was out of stock at DJI (DJI, n.d.-a) [V]. Global shutter solves skew on whip pans, which is rarely the limiting problem on a sideline [I]. |

## 7. Where consumer wireless is fine, and where it is not

| Use | Consumer or prosumer link (Hollyland, Accsoon, DJI) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Film set video village, focus pulling, director's monitor | **Fine** | Picture is for viewing; the recording is in camera [I]. |
| Documentary with a field producer watching | **Fine** | Same reason [I]. |
| Corporate event, house screens (IMAG) | **Usually fine** if delay is acceptable to the room | 25 to 80 ms is 1.5 to 5 frames at 59.94 [I]; lip sync on big screens can show it [I]. |
| High school or college stream | **Often fine** with the producer's approval and a tested route | Smaller RF environment; check venue Wi-Fi and coordinator [I]. |
| Network or league sideline | **Only if the show's EIC and frequency coordinator accept it**; otherwise use the vendor's RF | League coordination rules, crowd blocking, and broadcast RF built for this environment (NFL Football Operations, n.d.; Teradek, 2024; VidOvation, n.d.) [V]. |

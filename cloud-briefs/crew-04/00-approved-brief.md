# Brief: Megalodon: Cinema-Look Camera Rig (proposed CREW-04)

**Status:** approved 2026-10-21 for build. Public; held for a qualified reviewer.

## 1. Description

A learner can explain each part of a shallow-depth-of-field wireless gimbal camera (body, lens, gimbal,
wireless video, power, audio, control), how the parts trade off, how the rig joins a live sports broadcast
(RF or fiber path, receivers, frame sync, shading, tally, intercom, return video, timecode, frequency
coordination), how to troubleshoot it under a live clock, and which compromises are fine for film and
documentary work but not for a network sideline.

## 2. Outline (draft)

1. Why the look: depth of field, sensor size, and the 2020 broadcast debut.
2. The camera body: sensor, rolling versus global shutter, codecs, heat, outputs.
3. Lenses: speed, focal length, focus, the cost of very fast glass.
4. The gimbal: payload, balance, modes, running a sideline.
5. Wireless video: consumer links versus broadcast RF, latency, range, crowded bands, frequency coordination.
6. Joining the show: receivers, frame sync, shading and color match, tally, comms, return feed, timecode.
7. Troubleshooting: symptom, cause, check, fix; the pre-show checklist.
8. Builds and compromises: three tiers with dated prices; what to own and what to rent for
   each part. The business of renting gear out is its own course, CREW-06.

About 8 sections, 30 to 34 lessons.
